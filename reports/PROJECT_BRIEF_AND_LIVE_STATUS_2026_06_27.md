3. Research queue fragmentation:
   - old hourly queue and new priority queue both exist.

4. Live/backtest parity bugs:
   - closed-candle issues were found and fixed for key research modules, but every candidate still needs parity gate before risk.

5. Heavy research on 1GB live VPS:
   - timeouts and OOM risk happened; all future specs must be bounded/memory-safe.

6. Old positive numbers are not automatically valid:

7. Scanner cards are not trading decisions:
   - `RANGE scan: found=8` is useful discovery, but live entries still depend on allowlist, regime, touch, RR, cooldown, and risk.
